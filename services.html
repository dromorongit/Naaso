Umrah Packages

Blessed journey packages to the holy cities of Islam

Overview

Umrah is a sacred pilgrimage to Makkah that can be performed at any time of the year. Our Umrah packages offer a spiritual journey with maximum comfort and convenience. Whether you're performing Umrah alone or with family, we tailor our packages to meet your specific needs.

Unlike Hajj, Umrah does not have specific dates and can be performed throughout the year. This flexibility makes it ideal for those who wish to experience the spiritual benefits of pilgrimage at their convenience.

Package Options

  • Economy Package: Standard hotel accommodation, basic amenities
  • Standard Package: 4-star hotel near Haram, breakfast included
  • Premium Package: 5-star hotel within walking distance, full board
  • Family Package: Customized for families with special assistance
  • Ramadan Package: Special Umrah during the blessed month

What's Included

  • Saudi Arabia Umrah Visa
  • Round-trip flights
  • Hotel accommodation in Makkah
  • Airport transfers
  • Transportation to religious sites
  • Umrah guide services
  • 24/7 support in Saudi Arabia

Best Time to Perform Umrah

The best time for Umrah is during the winter months (October to March) when the weather is cooler. Ramadan is also a popular time for Umrah as it carries greater spiritual reward. Book early to secure the best rates and accommodations.

Saudi Visa Services

Hajj, Umrah, and Tourist visa processing

Visa Types We Process

Hajj Visa

For pilgrims performing Hajj. Requires sponsorship from licensed travel agency. Processing time: 2-4 weeks.

Umrah Visa

For pilgrims performing Umrah. Can be used throughout the year except during Hajj season. Processing time: 1-2 weeks.

Tourist Visa

For tourism and leisure travel to Saudi Arabia. Available for Ghanaian passport holders. Processing time: 3-5 business days.

Business Visa

For business travelers attending meetings, conferences, or trade events. Requires invitation from Saudi company.

General Requirements

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Completed visa application form
  • Recent passport-sized photographs (white background)
  • Proof of accommodation in Saudi Arabia
  • Round-trip flight booking
  • Proof of financial means
  • Yellow fever vaccination certificate (if applicable)
  • Meningitis vaccination for pilgrims

Processing Time

  • Tourist Visa: 3-5 business days
  • Umrah Visa: 7-14 business days
  • Hajj Visa: 14-21 business days
  • Express Processing: Available for select visa types (additional fees apply)

Important Notice

Visa requirements and processing times may change. We recommend applying well in advance of your intended travel date. Contact us for the most current information and personalized assistance.

Dubai & UAE Visa Services

Visa Types We Process

Tourist Visa

30-day and 90-day tourist visas available. Single and multiple entry options. Processing: 3-5 business days.

Transit Visa

Work Visa

Family Visa

China Visa Services

Business and tourist visa processing for China

Visa Types We Process

Tourist Visa (L)

For travelers visiting China for tourism. Single, double, and multiple entry options available.

Business Visa (M)

For business travelers attending meetings, trade fairs, or commercial activities in China.

Work Visa (Z)

For foreign nationals who have obtained employment in China. Requires work permit.

Student Visa (X)

For international students admitted to Chinese educational institutions.

Requirements

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months, at least 2 blank pages)
  • Completed visa application form
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Proof of accommodation in China
  • Round-trip flight booking
  • For business: Invitation letter from Chinese company
  • For tourist: Itinerary and financial proof
  • Travel insurance (recommended)

Processing Time

  • Regular Processing: 7-10 business days
  • Express Processing: 3-5 business days (additional fee)
  • Urgent Processing: 1-2 business days (additional fee)

Ghana to China
