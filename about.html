Our Story

Building Trust Since 2022

Naaso Travels and Tours was founded with a singular vision: to make the sacred journey of Hajj and Umrah accessible, comfortable, and spiritually fulfilling for pilgrims from Ghana and across West Africa.

Over the past 4 years, we have grown from a small local agency to one of Ghana's most trusted names in pilgrimage travel. Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to providing personalized service that addresses every aspect of your journey.

What started as a focus on Hajj and Umrah has expanded to include comprehensive visa services, flight bookings, and travel arrangements to destinations worldwide. Today, we continue to uphold our founding principles of integrity, excellence, and unwavering commitment to our clients.

Our Expertise

Excellence in Hajj & Umrah Coordination

With over 4 years of experience coordinating Hajj and Umrah pilgrimages, Naaso Travels has developed unmatched expertise in managing every aspect of the holy journey.

Our team has successfully guided over 5,000 pilgrims through the spiritual journey of a lifetime. We understand the unique requirements, cultural sensitivities, and logistical challenges that come with pilgrimage travel.

From the moment you express interest until you return home safely, our dedicated team is with you every step of the way. We handle visa processing, flight bookings, accommodations in Makkah and Madinah, transportation, group coordination, and provide experienced guides throughout your pilgrimage.
